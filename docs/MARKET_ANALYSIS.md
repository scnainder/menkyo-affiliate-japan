# Market Analysis - Menkyo Affiliate Japan

## Japanese Driver's License Market Overview

### Market Size
- **Annual Driver License Applicants**: ~3.5 million
- **Jidousha Gakkou Students**: ~2.8 million annually
- **Market Value**: ¥500+ billion annually

### Target Demographics

| Demographic | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Age | 16-65 years old |
| Primary | 16-30 (65% of market) |
| Occupation | Students, Workers, Career Changers |
| Tech Savviness | Medium to High |
| Mobile Usage | High (85%+ smartphone users) |

### Jidousha Gakkou Ecosystem

#### Types of Schools
1. **Large Chain Schools** (全国展開系)
   - Highest commission rates (5-10%)
   - Best brand recognition
   - Multiple locations

2. **Regional Schools** (地域密着型)
   - Moderate commission (3-7%)
   - Strong local presence
   - Personalized services

3. **Budget Options** (予算型)
   - Lower commission (2-5%)
   - Growing segment
   - Price-sensitive market

### Current Market Gaps

1. **Information Asymmetry**
   - Drivers lack comparison tools
   - Limited online reviews
   - Difficult school discovery process

2. **Trust Issues**
   - No centralized rating system
   - Quality varies by school
   - Hidden costs concerns

3. **Digital Transformation**
   - Most schools use basic websites
   - Limited online booking
   - Poor mobile experience

## Affiliate Program Economics

### Typical Commission Structure
- **Successful Registration**: ¥2,000 - ¥5,000 per lead
- **Enrollment Completion**: ¥5,000 - ¥15,000 per student
- **Referral Bonus**: ¥1,000 - ¥3,000 per new affiliate

### Revenue Model
```
Total Revenue = Number of Registrations × Average Commission × Affiliate Success Rate
```

### Example Projection (Year 1)
```
Conservative:
- 500 registrations × ¥3,000 = ¥1,500,000
- Less affiliate payouts (70%) = ¥450,000 net

Moderate:
- 2,000 registrations × ¥4,000 = ¥8,000,000
- Less affiliate payouts (70%) = ¥2,400,000 net

Optimistic:
- 5,000 registrations × ¥5,000 = ¥25,000,000
- Less affiliate payouts (70%) = ¥7,500,000 net
```
